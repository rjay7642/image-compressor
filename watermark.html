Add Watermarks to Images

Protect your images by adding a text watermark in seconds. This tool is great for photographers, designers, and creators who want to add branding before publishing online. All processing is done locally in your browser.

Watermarked images help reduce unauthorized reuse and keep your brand visible when images are shared on social media or blogs.

Recommended Workflow

  • Resize images for the target platform.
  • Add a consistent brand watermark.
  • Compress before uploading to speed up loading.

Related Tools

Start with Image Resizer, then compress using the Image Compressor. For format changes, use Image Converter.

How It Works

  • Select your image from your device.
  • Choose your preferred settings and click "Watermark".
  • Download the watermarked image instantly.

Browser-based processing keeps your files private and avoids uploads. This also makes watermarking faster and more reliable.

For best results, resize before watermarking so the placement stays consistent across images.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I add a text watermark?

Yes, you can add a text watermark in seconds.

Are images uploaded to a server?

No, watermarking happens locally in your browser.

Which formats are supported?

JPG, PNG, and WebP are supported.

Should I watermark before or after resizing?

Resize first, then add the watermark.
