Split PDF Pages Easily

Split a PDF into individual pages or extract only the pages you need. This tool keeps everything local in your browser, which means your documents stay private. It is perfect for submitting only selected pages or organizing large PDFs.

Smaller PDFs are faster to download and easier to index, which can help SEO when you publish document libraries or resources on your site.

Common Scenarios

  • Extract specific pages for forms or submissions.
  • Break large PDFs into smaller, organized parts.
  • Share only what is relevant to clients or teams.

Related Tools

For combining files, use PDF Merge. To shrink file size, try PDF Compress.

How It Works

  • Select your PDF from your device.
  • Choose your preferred settings and click "Split".
  • Download the split pages instantly.

Everything runs in your browser, so documents are never uploaded to a server. This keeps your data private and speeds up processing.

For larger files, compress the output pages to reduce download size.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PDF split used for?

It lets you extract or split pages from a PDF.

Are files uploaded to a server?

No, splitting happens locally in your browser.

Can I split specific pages only?

Yes, you can choose the pages you need.

Is it free to use?

Yes, it is completely free.
