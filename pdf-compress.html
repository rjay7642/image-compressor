Compress PDF Files Online

Reduce PDF file size without installing anything. The compression runs in your browser so your documents never leave your device. This is great for email attachments, document uploads, and improving download speed.

Smaller PDFs load faster and provide a better user experience. If your site hosts downloadable PDFs, optimizing them can improve SEO performance and reduce bandwidth costs.

Why Compress PDFs

  • Faster downloads and smoother sharing.
  • Smaller attachments for email.
  • Better performance for document-heavy pages.

How It Works

  • Select your PDF from your device.
  • Choose your preferred settings and click "Compress".
  • Download the compressed PDF instantly.

Browser-based processing keeps files private and avoids uploads. This also makes compression faster and more reliable.

For larger documents, compress again after merging or splitting to keep file sizes small.
