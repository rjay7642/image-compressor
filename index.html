About This Image Compressor Tool

This online image compressor allows you to reduce the file size of your images instantly - without installing any software. You can upload JPG, PNG, and WebP formats and compress them directly in your browser. Reducing image size helps speed up website load times, lowers bandwidth usage, and optimizes images for social media and email sharing. It's free, secure, and fast.

How It Works

  • Select your image or drag & drop it into the upload area.
  • Choose a target size (e.g., 50KB, 100KB).
  • Click "Compress Image".
  • Download the optimized file.

Why Image Compression Matters

Large images slow down websites, hurt SEO rankings, and increase mobile data usage. Optimized images improve performance, user experience, and Google search visibility.
