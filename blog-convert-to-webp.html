Why WebP Is Better and How to Convert Images

Practical tips to optimize images for speed and SEO.

WebP is a modern image format designed for smaller file sizes without noticeable quality loss. It helps improve loading speed and Core Web Vitals.

Benefits of WebP

  • Smaller file sizes than JPG or PNG for most images.
  • Support for transparency and animation.
  • Faster loading for mobile and desktop users.

How to Convert

Upload your image to the Image Converter and select WebP as the output. Then use the Image Compressor if you want to reduce the size even further.

When to Keep JPG or PNG

If you need legacy compatibility or specific transparency handling, JPG and PNG are still safe choices. The best approach is to test and compare.
