Merge PDF Files Online

Combine multiple PDFs into a single file without installing software. This tool runs fully in your browser so your files never leave your device. It is ideal for joining invoices, proposals, or scanned pages.

A single merged PDF is easier to share, store, and index. When you keep documents organized, it also improves search visibility for document libraries and resource pages.

Best Use Cases

  • Combine scanned pages into one document.
  • Join multiple reports or proposals.
  • Organize PDFs for easy sharing and archiving.

Related Tools

Need to split or compress? Use PDF Split or PDF Compress. Convert images to PDFs with Image to PDF.

How It Works

  • Select your PDFs from your device.
  • Choose your preferred settings and click "Merge".
  • Download the merged PDF instantly.

Because everything runs locally in your browser, files are processed quickly and never uploaded to a server. This keeps your content private while still giving you professional results.

For best performance, compress large PDFs after merging to keep file sizes manageable.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I merge PDFs?

Select multiple PDFs and merge them into one file.

Are PDFs uploaded to a server?

No, merging happens locally in your browser.

Does order matter?

Yes, PDFs are merged in the order you select.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no fixed limit, but performance depends on your device.
